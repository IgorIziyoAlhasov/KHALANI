# Research Charter v0.1

**Date:** 2026-11-22  
**Project:** KHALANI / Neo-Khalani  
**Direction:** B — Naturalistic Neo-Khalani

## Scope

Document identifiable linguistic material in *StarCraft* primary sources and related official publications, then develop a clearly distinguishable community language around the evidence.

## Commitments

- Preserve unmodified transcriptions and textual forms from their cited sources.
- Represent every claimed meaning as a separately attributable claim.
- Do not infer morphemes simply from resemblance to English translations.
- Treat factional and cultural context as data, not proof of mutually unintelligible dialects.
- Avoid inventing canonical vocabulary, orthographic rules or historical sound changes.
- Make uncertainty first-class, including unresolved or conflicting interpretations.
- Keep fan additions clearly and consistently labeled.

## Deliverables

Evidence register, source-specific attestations, comparative translation notes, phonetic observations, structural hypotheses, and an explicit Phase I verification report.

## Publication boundaries

The long-term language is a fan-created elaboration, **not** the official Protoss language. Retain citations, respect source licensing and avoid including copyrighted game files without permission.
