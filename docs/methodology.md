# Methodology and Evidence Policy v0.1

## 1. Separate source authority from analytical confidence

**Source tiers:**
- **S1**: primary game audio, scripts, in-game text, game data or original official manuals.
- **S2**: official supplementary publications or identifiable developer statements.
- **S3**: secondary transcriptions, encyclopedias and scholarly discussion.
- **S4**: explicitly creative fan language and proposed Neo-Khalani material.

**Interpretation states:**
- **verified**: directly corroborated by an identifiable source and locator.
- **strongly_supported**: independently repeated evidence supports the interpretation.
- **tentative**: plausible analysis, not established.
- **unresolved**: insufficient evidence or contradictory interpretation.
- **constructed**: authored for Neo-Khalani, never asserted to be canon.

A source can be S1 while its meaning is unresolved. A translation can be reliable while its morpheme-by-morpheme segmentation is wholly unknown.

## 2. Attestation unit

One attestation = one occurrence of a form at a specific source location. A lexical headword may link multiple attestations but must never replace the underlying event.

Keep distinct fields for source text, audio transcription, official spelling (if verified), translation claims, speaker, faction, context and source locator.

## 3. Verification protocol

1. Record discovery lead and citation.
2. Find independently accessible primary source and exact locator (game, mission/unit, file path, timestamp, page).
3. Copy the original form without normalization, including capitalization and punctuation.
4. Record published translation and its source separately; do not silently generate one.
5. Have a second pass assess transcription certainty and alternative hearings.
6. Only then promote an attestation's verification status.
7. Log unconfirmed claims and missing materials as negative findings, not proof of absence.

## 4. Hypothesis procedure

Every hypothesis has an ID, competing explanations, supporting and counter-evidence, scope, and confidence. If a pattern such as `En Taro X` recurs, the recurrence supports a formulaic construction, not necessarily individual meanings for `En` and `Taro`.

## 5. Copyright and repeatability

Prefer source URLs, file paths, checksums, edition identifiers, and timecodes. Do not upload proprietary audio, scripts or artwork in bulk without permission. Cite modest excerpts when necessary.

## 6. Quality gate

Schema validation and corpus-reference checks should be automated. Scientific judgments (audio hearing, semantics and primary-source authenticity) require human review.
