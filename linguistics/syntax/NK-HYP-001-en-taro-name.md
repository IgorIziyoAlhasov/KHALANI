# NK-HYP-001 — The *En Taro + named honoree* formula

**Status:** Strongly supported **as a recurring surface construction**, not as a fully productive grammatical rule.

## Observations

Official Blizzard publications attest both `En Taro Adun` (previous Passes 006–007) and `En Taro Tassadar` (Pass 008). This shows the two-word preamble can precede at least two different proper names.

## Competing interpretations

- **H1 (favored):** a conventional ceremonial formula with a replaceable honoree slot.
- **H2:** two conventionalized independent phrases, with analogy but no productive template.
- **H3:** larger discourse formulae whose surface similarity may not reflect conventional compositional grammar.

Evidence currently favors H1 over H2 descriptively, but cannot yet rule out restricted lexicalization.

## What remains unknown

- Whether `en` and `taro` are separate meaningful morphemes.
- What morphosyntactic category `En Taro` belongs to.
- Whether the construction allows ordinary nouns, groups, abstract concepts, or only certain distinguished names.
- Whether orthographic case corresponds to a grammatical distinction.
- Whether `En Taro Zeratul` is independently attested in an original accessible Blizzard source; secondary references exist.

## Neo-Khalani impact

Do **not** freely coin `En Taro X` with unattested names as canonical. Neo-Khalani may adopt a productive construction in a later explicitly `constructed` grammar proposal, separate from the canonical corpus.
