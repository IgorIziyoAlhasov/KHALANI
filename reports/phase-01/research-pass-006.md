## Source-critical interpretation

These are official *written uses*, not an official phonetic transcription. Accented `Adún` in Spanish text is likely a localization convention. It cannot be used on its own to assert Khalani phonological stress. Similarly the variable capitalization of `taro` is not grammatical evidence.

**Permitted claim:** Blizzard published these closely related formulae.  
**Not permitted:** `en` = “in”; `taro` = “honor”; acute accent is Khalani orthography; one version is globally normative.

The earlier seed `NK-ATT-0001` remains an unverified *secondary-sourced discovery record*. These are new, distinct official occurrences and must not be silently substituted for it.

## Practical editor-research finding

Blizzard's StarCraft II 5.0 notes document the ability to override editor locale text files (`EditorStrings.txt`, `EditorCatalogStrings.txt`, `EditorCategoryStrings.txt`): https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23482838/starcraft-ii-5-0-patch-notes . These files govern editor UI strings and are not automatically the in-game Conversations translations.

This reinforces the need to connect precise game data IDs to their *corresponding* localized strings rather than treating any nearby English voice response as a translation.
