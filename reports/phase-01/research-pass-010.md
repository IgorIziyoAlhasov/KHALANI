# Research Pass 010 — Unity and justice expression audit

**Date:** 2026-11-27

## A. Khassar de Templari

The independent licensed tabletop game publisher **Archon Studio** prints `Khassar de Templari!` in its *StarCraft High Templar* product description:
https://archon-studio.com/shop/products/starcraft-tabletop-miniatures-game/starcraft-high-templar

**Status:** direct publisher-written occurrence verified. A licensed publisher is an **S2 supplementary source**, not equivalent to Blizzard's original in-game text and not sufficient to establish literal translation.

The community-indexed SC2 Protoss voice lines report:
- `HighTemplar_Ready00.ogg`: `Khassar de Templari!`
- `Zealot_Yes04.ogg`: `Khassar de templari!`

Source: https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/StarCraft_II_unit_quotations/Protoss

A community *Heroes of the Storm* quotes page associates `From order comes justice` with the original High Templar utterance, but **no direct Blizzard English translation entry was verified**: https://heroesofthestorm.wiki.gg/wiki/Tassadar/Quotes

A 2012 fan-language analysis records further competing segmentations: https://khalani-language.blogspot.com/2012/03/making-it-fit.html . This documents fan orthographic disagreement, not canon spelling.

## B. Ki nala atum

The same secondary unit index reports adjacent Mothership lines:
- `Mothership_What02.ogg`: `As all are one.`
- `Mothership_What03.ogg`: `Ki nala atum.`

The Khalani wiki glosses the latter as `We are as one`. **Neither indexed proximity nor fan gloss demonstrates direct original-game translation.**

## C. NK-HYP-002 — atum and unity

Compare reported forms `Ki nala atum`, `Na Adan Atum`, and `Uhn ore'ki atum`. The recurrence of `atum` is a **candidate form-level relationship**.

No independent translation of the element is demonstrable yet. The evidence cannot justify adopting `atum` = “one”, “unity” or a specific grammatical marker.
