# Research Pass 008 — Testing name substitution in *En Taro*

**Date:** 2026-11-26  
**Objective:** Find primary publisher evidence of `En Taro` with a honoree other than Adun.

Earlier passes established official written `En Taro Adun` material. **This gives independent publication evidence for two different name fillers.**

## Hypothesis

`NK-HYP-001`: `En Taro + [name of honoree]` functions as a recurring ceremonial formula. The **surface substitution** Adun → Tassadar is strongly supported by official-source examples.

### Not established

- `En` independently means “in” or `Taro` independently means “honor”.
- All proper names can be substituted, or that the construction extends to groups and abstract nouns.
- A canonical direct English translation provided by these sources.
- Native Khalani phonology or stress from the written forms.
- `En Taro Zeratul` as primary-source verified in this pass (secondary claims found).
